61. create a logical volume
a. lvcreate -n lvname -l 32 vgname

2. create a striped logical volume.
a. lvcreate -n lvname --type raid0 -l 256 -I 64K vgname /dev/sdb /dev/sdc

3. split a volume group
a. vgsplit old_vg new_vg

4. remove a disk from volume group
a. vgreduce vgname /dev/sdc

5. move all exntents from one pv to another
a. pvmove /dev/sdb /dev/sdc
   pvmove source destiny

6. create a mirrored logical volume
a. lvcreate -n lvname -l 32  m 1 datavg /dev/sdb /dev/sdc

7. create a mirror to an existing linear lv
8. remove last pv from the vg
a. vgremove vgname 
   pvremove /dev/sdb

9. fix insufficient extent issue for a logical volume.
a. vgextend vgname /dev/sde
